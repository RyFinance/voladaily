How to read Voladaily

Every trading day ahead gets two forecasts: which way the market leans and how far it is likely to move. The second is the reliable one.

The numbers

+0.11%
Expected return, close to close. Small by nature: a few tenths of a percent on a normal day.
±0.81%
Typical size of the move, up or down. This is where the model has real skill.
55% up
Chance the session closes higher. 50% is a coin flip; most days sit between 48% and 58%.
80% range
Four days out of five should close inside it.
High-risk session: a big scheduled event (Fed, CPI, jobs, earnings) or an unusually wide expected move.

How it works

Size comes from a realized-volatility model (HAR) fed by the VIX curve and the economic calendar: CPI, jobs, Fed days, options expiry, elections and, for single stocks, earnings dates from SEC filings.

Direction is a heavily shrunk regression on effects that are well documented: short-term reversal, pre-Fed drift, turn of the month and the volatility risk premium.

Both are tested walk-forward since 2010, on data the model never saw. The track record at the bottom of each page shows how it has done, including where it does no better than "always up".

A forecast of odds, not a promise. Not investment advice.
